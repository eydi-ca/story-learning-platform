Chapter 3
The Town of Integers

As I stepped through the portal, I found myself in a circular town.

System Message:
"Welcome to the Town of Integers."
"The left side is the Negative District."
"The right side is in the Positive District."
"At the center stands the Zero Portal, connected to the Counting Forest."

----

After a few moments, fractions and decimals had entered the town, causing chaos. Numbers wandered along the roads, confused and misplaced.

System Message:
"Integers consist of negative numbers, zero and positive numbers."
"Arrange the numbers based on their correct placement in the town, and remove all non-integer numbers to solve the chaos."

-----

I fixed the town step by step.
I placed 0 at the center.
I guided the positive number to the right.
I led negative numbers to the left.
And I removed the Fractions and decimals from the town.
The paths aligned. The town became calm.
A red gem descended into my hands.

System Message:
"Sacred Gem Acquired: Z"

----
