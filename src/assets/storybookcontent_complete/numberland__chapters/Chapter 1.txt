Chapter 1
The Counting Forest (N)

I landed in a quiet forest. At its center stood a broken stone altar with dark, empty slots. Scattered around it were glowing numbers:


System Message:
"Counting Numbers detected: Positive whole numbers only."

System Alert:
"Forest altar requires counting numbers to activate."

------

I realized the problem immediately.
Counting numbers start at 1 and go up. No Zero.
No negatives. No fractions.
I placed 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 into the alter.

System Instruction:
"Select and place only counting numbers"

-----

After completing the task, a light flowed through the forest.
A green gem rose from the altar and floated into my hand.

System Message:
Sacred Gem Acquired: N.

-----
